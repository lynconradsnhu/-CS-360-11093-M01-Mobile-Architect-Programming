# -CS-360-11093-M01-Mobile-Architect-Programming

##Briefly summarize the requirements and goals of the app you developed. What user needs was this app designed to address?

The purpose of the application was to enable the user to create, modify, and delete events and view and receive notifications for those events. These features were designed to address the user needs of organizing and receiving reminders for events. 

##What screens and features were necessary to support user needs and produce a user-centered UI for the app? How did your UI designs keep users in mind? Why were 
your designs successful?

The major screens of the application included the login screen, the events dashboard, the add event screen, the event modification and event deletion screens, and the notification screen for permitting notifications and viewing upcoming notifications. 

##How did you approach the process of coding your app? What techniques or strategies did you use? How could those techniques or strategies be applied in the future? How did you test to ensure your code was functional? Why is this process important, and what did it reveal?

I coded my app by first defining the requirements and creating a design document, then building out the core activities using best coding practices and documentation. Then, I added the database and connected the activities and enhanced the UI. I also did research to check if there were better ways I could be implementing an idea. Throughout the development process I tested, reviewed code, and built the project regularly to check for mistakes. Often this process revealed opportunities for improvements. These are standard approaches that could be applied to most projects, even outside of mobile development.

##Consider the full app design and development process from initial planning to finalization. Where did you have to innovate to overcome a challenge?

My design document was quite thorough, although I did innovate a bit to ensure that notification permissions were configurable and added on Android notifications to support the purpose of the app in the case that SMS notifications were not permitted by the user.

##In what specific component of your mobile app were you particularly successful in demonstrating your knowledge, skills, and experience?

The events dashboard I felt showed by accumulated knowledge as a developer. It showed all the immediate information the user needed with easy to spot buttons for navigating and performing further actions.
